About Project

Waaree Energies entrusted us with the construction of a 25 Km road including shoulder works at Thattaparai, Tamil Nadu. Executed with a total budget of ?3.89 Crores, the project aimed to support logistical access and infrastructure development for their renewable energy operations in the region.

Scope of Work

  • Earthwork and subgrade preparation across the entire 25 Km stretch.
  • Laying of bituminous layers and shoulder development.
  • Quality control, compaction, and surface finishing in compliance with industry standards.
