Our Legacy

AARAA Infrastructure Pvt Ltd is a premier PAN India construction and engineering company headquartered in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. Since 2012, we have delivered turnkey EPC solutions across industrial, commercial, institutional, infrastructure, and renewable energy sectors, serving clients from Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, and Maharashtra.

Welcome to AARAA Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd.

Your Partner in Civil Construction excellence.

To deliver safe, sustainable, and high-quality civil engineering solutions aligned with our core values and client expectations.

AARAA Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. (FKA AARAA Engineering Enterprises), established in 2012 in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, is a premier Turnkey Civil Contracting firm. We provide end-to-end solutions in Civil, Structural, Architectural, Interior, MEP, and HVAC services. Our projects span Warehouses/PEBs, Educational Institutions, Commercial Hubs, and Infrastructure works.

We deliver educational infrastructure projects with student-friendly and future-ready environments. From classrooms and laboratories to sports and administrative blocks, our designs emphasize safety, utility, and long-term value aligned with institutional goals.
