Renewables & Solar EPC

Aaraa Infrastructure actively supports India's transition to a green future as a leading Solar EPC contractor. We execute megawatt-scale utility projects, including comprehensive 70.4 Megawatt hybrid energy developments. Our specialized engineering teams manage the entire project lifecycle—from establishing expansive 80-acre site boundaries to the precision installation of thousands of structural piles and Module Mounting Structures (MMS). Partner with us to develop robust, grid-connected renewable infrastructure that powers tomorrow.

Key Capabilities:


Turnkey Solar EPC Solutions: Complete execution from site preparation and 1.4-meter deep pile foundations to precise 12-degree Module Mounting Structure (MMS) alignment and grid connection.

Utility-Scale Execution: Proven capability in managing massive developments, including 70.4 MW blocks involving over 6,180 structural piles for leading developers like Continuum Green Energy.

Advanced Civil & Drilling Engineering: Specialized in executing complex civil works in hilly and rocky terrains utilizing heavy-duty DTH (Down-The-Hole) drilling equipment and M25 grade concrete securing.

Comprehensive Electrical Infrastructure: Engineering extensive DC/AC trench networks, incorporating multi-layer safety protocols with sand beds, earth rods, and precise SMB/Inverter installations spanning thousands of meters.

Solar EPC Technical Capabilities

We execute massive utility-scale projects across India. For example, we manage extensive 70.4 Megawatt hybrid blocks spread across 80+ acres, handling everything from foundation engineering to the installation of over 6,100 structural piles and precision solar module alignments.

We utilize advanced DTH (Down-The-Hole) drilling machines to penetrate hilly and rocky terrain, securely casting foundation piles to a 1.4-meter depth with M25 grade concrete to ensure structural integrity where standard augers fail.

We construct rigorous DC/AC trench networks—often exceeding 6,700 running meters per block. Our protocol involves meticulous multi-layer engineering, including specialized sand beds, precise cable laying, earth rods, warning tapes, and protective brick layers before final backfilling.
