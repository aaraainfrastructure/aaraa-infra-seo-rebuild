About Project

Successfully completed the construction of a Shell Petrol Bunk located in Wagholi, Pune City. The project involved end-to-end execution of civil, structural, and finishing works to deliver a fully operational fuel station. The total project budget was ?1.2 Crores.

Scope of Work

  • Site development and excavation works for underground tanks and foundations.
  • Construction of canopy structure, RCC works, and pump island platforms.
  • Execution of MEP (Mechanical, Electrical & Plumbing) installations for station operations.
  • Driveway and paver block laying for vehicle movement and customer access.
  • Finishing works including painting, signage installation, and external lighting setup.
