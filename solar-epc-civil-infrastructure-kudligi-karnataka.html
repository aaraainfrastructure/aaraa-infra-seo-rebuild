About the Solar EPC Project at Kudligi

AARAA Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. has been entrusted with executing civil infrastructure works for the 132.26 MW Wind–Solar Hybrid Project at Kudligi, Karnataka, awarded by Continuum Green Energy Ltd..

This hybrid renewable energy development integrates wind and solar generation to optimize power output and grid stability. Our role focuses on building the essential civil backbone required for long-term operational efficiency and structural reliability.

Scope of Civil Infrastructure Works

  • Site grading and earthworks to establish stable platforms for solar arrays and associated structures.
  • Piling and foundations for MMS structures and equipment platforms.
  • Internal roads and drainage systems to ensure efficient site mobility and storm-water management.
  • Perimeter development and access roads for secure and structured site operations.
  • Quality control and finishing works executed in strict adherence to project specifications and safety standards.

Delivering Infrastructure for Hybrid Renewable Energy

The Wind–Solar Hybrid model requires high-precision civil execution to ensure alignment, structural durability, and operational stability. AARAA Infrastructure combines technical expertise and disciplined project management to deliver safe, compliant, and future-ready infrastructure solutions.

This project further strengthens our portfolio in the Infrastructure – Renewable Energy segment across India.
