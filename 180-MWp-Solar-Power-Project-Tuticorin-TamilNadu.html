AARAA Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. is playing a key role in enabling large-scale clean energy generation in Tamil Nadu through the 180 MWp Solar Power Project at Tuticorin, delivering robust civil infrastructure that supports long-term solar performance.

About the 180 MWp Solar Power Project

AARAA Infrastructure has been entrusted by Waaree Energies Ltd. to execute the civil infrastructure development works for their upcoming 180 MWp Solar Power Project in Tuticorin, Tamil Nadu. This engagement underscores our expertise in delivering reliable, large-format infrastructure for utility-scale solar parks.

From earthworks and internal roads to robust foundations and drainage networks, every element is engineered to support the performance and longevity of the solar assets installed on site.

Scope of Civil Infrastructure Works

  • Site grading and earthworks to prepare a stable platform for the solar park.
  • Internal roads and drainage to enable smooth movement of equipment and safe storm-water management.
  • Foundations and pedestals for MMS/structures and equipment platforms supporting solar modules and critical systems.
  • Perimeter development and approach access including boundary treatments and connectivity to external roads.
  • Quality control, compaction, and finishing in strict line with client specifications and project standards.

Building Infrastructure for Renewable Energy

Solar power plants depend on precise civil engineering for long-term reliability. With disciplined project management and on-site execution, AARAA Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. ensures that all civil and ancillary works are delivered safely, on schedule, and in compliance with stringent quality benchmarks.

This project further strengthens our portfolio in the Infrastructure – Renewable Energy segment, supporting India's transition towards sustainable power generation.
