Frequently Asked Questions

A Solar EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) contractor handles the end-to-end execution of solar power plants, from design and structural engineering to procurement of materials and final grid integration.

A turnkey contract is a project delivery model where a single builder like AARAA Infrastructure manages the entire project lifecycle—from architectural design to final construction and fit-out—delivering a fully completed facility ready for use.

Pre Engineered Buildings (PEB) are highly efficient steel structures manufactured in a factory and assembled on-site. They are ideal for industrial warehouses, factories, and logistics parks due to their speed of construction and durability.

Industrial construction involves the development of specialized structural facilities such as manufacturing plants, chemical processing units, factories, and heavy-duty warehouses tailored to rigorous operational requirements.

It is the engineering and laying of high-durability concrete (RCC) or asphalt road networks within private infrastructure zones, industrial parks, and institutional campuses.

Commercial construction focuses on building structures intended for corporate or retail business, including office towers, shopping malls, corporate parks, and high-rise business centers.

Interior fit-outs involve transforming a bare structural shell into a fully functional and branded corporate space, encompassing MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing), partitions, flooring, ceilings, and custom furnishings.
