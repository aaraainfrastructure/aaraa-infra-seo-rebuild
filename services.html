Our Services

AARAA Infrastructure provides comprehensive construction and engineering services across India: general contracting, civil engineering, pre-engineered buildings (PEB), interior fit-out, MEP systems, HVAC, and project management. Our turnkey EPC execution covers industrial, commercial, institutional, infrastructure, and renewable energy projects.

Welcome to AARAA INFRASTRUCTURE PVT. LTD

We bring unmatched proficiency in executing Living Spaces, Commercial, Institutional, Industrial projects and Infrastructure projects. By sourcing superior-grade materials and employing experienced engineers, we ensure aesthetically appealing and structurally resilient outcomes. Our transparent operations, cost-efficient models, and customer-first approach make us the preferred choice for civil construction services.

We execute commercial construction projects with a focus on functionality, aesthetics, and operational efficiency. From retail complexes to office spaces, our structures are tailored to meet modern business needs, incorporating the latest MEP systems, ergonomic interiors, and sustainable building practices.

Our Core

As a leading general contractor, we manage all critical phases of construction—from pre-construction and sitework to final finishing. Our expertise covers Construction, Demolition, Onsite Carpentry, MEP, PEB, and HVAC systems. Leveraging advanced tools and technologies, we ensure timely, budget-friendly delivery without compromising safety or quality.
Warehouse & PEBs
We specialize in the construction of Warehouses and Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEBs), offering scalable, durable, and cost-efficient storage and logistics solutions. Our PEB structures are optimized for fast deployment and superior space utilization, using top-grade materials and advanced engineering standards to ensure long-term performance.
Educational Institutions
We deliver educational infrastructure projects with student-friendly and future-ready environments. From classrooms and laboratories to sports and administrative blocks, our designs emphasize safety, utility, and long-term value aligned with institutional goals.
Infrastructure
Our road construction services span from urban streets to industrial accessways, built to withstand heavy usage and environmental stress. We utilize modern techniques, premium asphalt, and concrete technologies to ensure smooth, durable, and safe transportation infrastructure.
Interior Contracts
We create custom interior environments that align with our clients' functional, aesthetic, and brand goals. From commercial and office spaces to institutional and industrial interiors, our fit-out services include partitions, ceilings, flooring, furniture, and finishes—delivered with precision, efficiency, and lasting quality.
MEP & HVAC
Our MEP (Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing) and HVAC solutions are designed to enhance building performance, energy efficiency, and occupant comfort. We integrate advanced systems for ventilation, air conditioning, lighting, water supply, and fire safety, ensuring full compliance with local and international standards.
General Contracting
We provide complete general contracting services for a wide range of civil, industrial, and commercial projects. From groundworks to finishing, we manage all aspects of construction with a focus on quality, cost control, and timely delivery—ensuring dependable execution at every stage.

Our Conclusion

As a multidisciplinary construction partner, we specialize not only as a general contractor but also across diverse sectors including Infrastructure, Institutional, Renewable Energy, Interior Fit-outs, and Fire Safety Systems. We expertly manage every critical phase—from pre-construction and site development to turnkey execution. Our capabilities span Construction, Demolition, Onsite Carpentry, MEP, PEB, and HVAC services. By leveraging cutting-edge tools and technologies, we deliver projects on time and within budget, while upholding the highest standards of safety and quality.
