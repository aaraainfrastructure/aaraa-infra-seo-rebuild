Have questions about our services, process, or expertise? Find quick answers to the most common inquiries about AARAA Infrastructure.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of construction services does AARAA Infrastructure offer in Chennai?
AARAA Infrastructure provides turnkey construction services for commercial, industrial, institutional, renewable energy, and interior fit-out projects. We manage every stage from design to delivery.
What is a turnkey construction project?
A turnkey construction project means we handle everything � from architectural design and approvals to civil execution and final handover � so you receive a fully completed, ready-to-use facility.
How much does it cost to build a commercial or industrial building?
Project costs vary based on size, location, materials, and specifications. For accurate estimates, please contact us at info@aaraainfrastructure.com or call +91 868 100 3111.
Do you handle institutional and hospital construction?
Yes, AARAA Infrastructure has successfully delivered high-quality construction for educational institutions, hospitals, clinics, and healthcare facilities across Tamil Nadu.
Does AARAA offer solar and renewable infrastructure services?
Yes. We design and execute rooftop and ground-mounted solar EPC projects, tailored to your energy needs and location. Contact us for a custom proposal.
Can AARAA Infrastructure construct PEB (Pre-Engineered Building) structures?
Absolutely. We specialize in PEB construction for warehouses, manufacturing units, logistics hubs, and factory spaces with structural efficiency and fast execution.
Can I request Vastu-compliant design plans?
Yes. We offer Vastu-aligned planning and layout services upon request, integrating traditional principles with contemporary architecture.
How do I get a project estimate or proposal?
Simply email us at info@aaraainfrastructure.com or call +91 868 100 3111. Our team will arrange a consultation and site visit as required.
Where is your office located?
