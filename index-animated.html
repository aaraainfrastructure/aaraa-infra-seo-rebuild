Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about AARAA Infrastructure and our services.

A Solar EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) contractor handles the end-to-end execution of solar power plants — from design and structural engineering to procurement of materials and final grid integration. AARAA Infrastructure delivers solar EPC projects across India with full-cycle accountability.

A turnkey contract is a project delivery model where a single builder — like AARAA Infrastructure — manages the entire project lifecycle, from architectural design to final construction and fit-out, delivering a fully completed facility ready for immediate use. It eliminates the need to coordinate multiple contractors.

AARAA Infrastructure delivers projects across Commercial, Industrial, Institutional, Infrastructure, Solar EPC & Renewables, PEB (Pre-Engineered Buildings), MEP & HVAC, and Interior Fit-Out sectors. Our multi-sector capability means a single partner can handle your entire project ecosystem.

A Pre-Engineered Building (PEB) is a steel structure designed, fabricated, and assembled using standardised components manufactured off-site. Key advantages include faster construction timelines (up to 30% quicker than conventional methods), lower material waste, design flexibility for large clear-span spaces, and significantly reduced construction costs — making it ideal for warehouses, factories, and large commercial facilities.

We operate across 25+ major cities in India including Chennai, Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad, Coimbatore, Kochi, and more. Our project portfolio spans Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Maharashtra, Telangana, Gujarat, Rajasthan, and multiple other states.

You can request a project quote by filling out our Quick Enquiry form, emailing us at aaraainfrastructure@gmail.com, or calling us directly. Our team typically responds within one business day with an initial assessment and project discussion.

AARAA Infrastructure follows ISO-compliant quality management systems and adheres to BIS (Bureau of Indian Standards), NBC (National Building Code), and relevant IS codes across all project categories. Our safety protocols include site-specific HSE (Health, Safety & Environment) plans, regular audits, certified safety personnel, and mandatory PPE compliance across all active sites.

Project timelines vary significantly based on scope, site conditions, approvals, and complexity. A small commercial interior fit-out may take 6–12 weeks, while a large industrial facility or institutional campus can take 12–36 months. AARAA provides a detailed project schedule during the pre-construction phase and uses milestone-based tracking to ensure timely delivery.
