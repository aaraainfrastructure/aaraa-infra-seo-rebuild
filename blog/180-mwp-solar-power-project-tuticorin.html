AARAA Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. is playing a key role in enabling large-scale clean energy generation in Tamil Nadu through the 180 MWp Solar Power Project at Tuticorin, delivering robust civil infrastructure that supports long-term solar performance.

About the 180 MWp Solar Power Project

AARAA Infrastructure has been entrusted by Waaree Energies Ltd. to execute civil infrastructure works for their 180 MWp Solar Power Project at Tuticorin, Tamil Nadu.

This project supports India's renewable energy expansion by delivering durable, performance-driven civil infrastructure that enables long-term solar generation efficiency.

Scope of Civil Infrastructure Works

  • Site grading and earthworks for solar park development
  • Foundations and pedestals for MMS structures and equipment
  • Internal roads and storm-water drainage systems
  • Perimeter development and access infrastructure
  • Quality control and finishing works as per project standards

Building Infrastructure for Utility-Scale Solar

Utility-scale solar projects require precision engineering and structured civil execution. AARAA Infrastructure ensures safe, compliant, and durable infrastructure delivery aligned with client specifications.
