About Project

Completed the construction of a large-scale industrial Work Shed for Meganath Alloys at Gummidipoondi. The project was designed to support heavy-duty fabrication and operational processes, executed within a budget of ?2.5 Crores.

Scope of Work

  • Design and erection of Pre-Engineered Building (PEB) structure suitable for industrial operations.
  • Civil works including foundation, flooring, and plinth beam construction.
  • Installation of roofing and cladding sheets with proper insulation and ventilation systems.
  • Provision of utility lines and basic MEP works for machinery setup and lighting.
  • External works including compound wall, approach road, and stormwater drainage.
