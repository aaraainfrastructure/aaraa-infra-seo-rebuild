Foray into Commercial & Industrial Projects:
Building on its engineering legacy, AARAA Infrastructure expanded into the commercial and industrial sectors with a focus on functionality, scalability, and compliance. Our initial projects in this segment were designed to support dynamic business operations�ranging from office spaces and retail hubs to warehouses and manufacturing facilities. Government Projects with TNPHC:
AARAA Infrastructure partnered with Tamil Nadu Police Housing Corporation (TNPHC) to undertake vital public infrastructure projects. These collaborations focused on delivering secure, durable, and cost-efficient facilities for law enforcement and government use. Through strict adherence to timelines, quality standards, and compliance protocols, AARAA strengthened its reputation as a trusted contractor in the government sector�reinforcing its capability to handle large-scale, public-interest developments.
