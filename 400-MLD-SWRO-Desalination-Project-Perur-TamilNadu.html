400 MLD SWRO Desalination Project – Perur, Tamil Nadu

In a region where water security is critical, the 400 MLD SWRO Desalination Project in Perur stands as a beacon of progress. AARAA Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. is proud to partner in this landmark initiative, ensuring a sustainable water future for Tamil Nadu.

A Landmark Step Toward Water Sustainability

Collaborating with global water technology leader VA Tech Wabag, the 400 MLD SWRO Desalination Plant in Perur, Tamil Nadu, represents a transformative approach to urban water management. This facility is not just an industrial project; it is a lifeline designed to address the pressing water needs of the region.

At AARAA Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd., our commitment to "Building The Future" is exemplified through our involvement in this project. We understand that reliable infrastructure is the backbone of community well-being, and this plant ensures that safe, potable water is accessible, reducing reliance on erratic monsoon patterns.

Why This Project Matters

  • Water Security: Provides a consistent 400 Million Litres per Day (MLD) of treated water.
  • Technological Excellence: Utilizes advanced Seawater Reverse Osmosis (SWRO) for efficient purification.
  • Community Impact: Directly contributes to the health and quality of life for millions in Chennai and surrounding areas.
  • Collaborative Success: A testament to the power of partnership between VA Tech Wabag and AARAA Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd.

AARAA Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd.'s Role: Civil & Allied Works

The success of a high-tech desalination plant relies heavily on the robustness of its physical structure. AARAA Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. has been entrusted with the execution of the Civil & Allied Works for this massive undertaking.

Our team brings precision engineering and project management expertise to the ground level, ensuring that every foundation, structure, and auxiliary facility meets the highest standards of durability and safety. We are proud to lay the concrete groundwork that allows advanced water technology to flourish.

Key Project Highlights

  • Location: Perur, Tamil Nadu
  • Capacity: 400 MLD
  • Scope: Civil & Allied Works by AARAA
  • Goal: Sustainable urban water supply

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the capacity of the Perur Desalination Project?

The project has a capacity of 400 MLD (Million Litres per Day), making it a significant contributor to the region's water supply.

Who are the key partners involved?

The project is a collaboration involving VA Tech Wabag as the technology lead and AARAA Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. executing the Civil & Allied Works.

How does this help Tamil Nadu's water security?

It diversifies the water source portfolio, reducing dependence on groundwater and rain, ensuring stability during droughts.

What is AARAA Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd.'s role?

AARAA is responsible for the critical Civil & Allied Works, ensuring the structural integrity required for the plant's operation.

Is SWRO technology sustainable?

Modern SWRO plants are designed for high energy recovery and efficiency, offering a long-term solution to water scarcity in coastal cities.
